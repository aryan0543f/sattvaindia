Omkar Pawar

Founder & Director

Sattva India

When we started Sattva India, our vision was simple but ambitious: to become the most reliable food partner for commercial kitchens across Maharashtra and beyond.

I've personally spent years understanding the challenges faced by hotel chefs, mess managers, and catering businesses. Inconsistent spice quality, unreliable suppliers, and fluctuating taste profiles were constant pain points.

At Sattva India, we've built our entire operation around consistency, transparency, and trust. Every batch we supply — whether it's 1kg of garam masala or 500kg of turmeric powder — goes through the same rigorous quality checks.

Our facility in Nigdi, Pune, is equipped with modern processing units, but more importantly, it's backed by a team that understands institutional food requirements. We don't just sell spices and sauces; we partner with you to ensure your kitchen never runs out of quality ingredients.

Today, we're proud to serve 100+ commercial clients — from boutique cafés to large factory canteens — and we're committed to maintaining the same level of quality and service as we grow.

Thank you for trusting Sattva India as your food partner. We look forward to serving you.

Omkar Pawar
